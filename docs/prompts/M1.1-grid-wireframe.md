## Goal
Draw thin grid lines between map tiles so individual tiles are easy to see.

## Acceptance
- Grid lines are drawn over the land, at tile boundaries only (no line around the map's outside beyond the normal tile edges; a separate story adds a border).
- Lines are thin and **constant on screen** (about 1 pixel) regardless of zoom, and subtle enough not to swamp the colors: start with a dark line at low alpha, and tell me the color and alpha you chose.
- Only grid lines for visible tiles are drawn (reuse the existing camera culling).
- When tiles are very small on screen the grid is hidden. Pick a threshold constant (suggest around 4 px per tile) as a named constant and tell me the value.
- Always on: **no toggle in this story**. A later story adds a settings screen with a checkbox.
- No change to `sim`, map data, or the loader.
- The `large` map (128x128) holds about the same FPS with the grid on. Report the FPS before and after, at min zoom and at a high zoom.

## Constraints
- `core` only. No new dependencies.
- If you add pure logic (for example "should the grid be visible at this zoom"), put it in a small testable method and add a unit test.
- Do not add keys, menus, or settings.
