## Goal
When hovering a tile, show its key numbers right on the map so the player can read them without looking away to the inspector. This is the MVP — just colored text, no fancy layout. Later iterations will evolve this into a richer visual (see the HUD vision in `docs/design.md`).

## Vocabulary
- **HUD**: the tile-attached overlay drawn on the map near the hovered tile. Not the inspector (the fixed upper-left panel).

## Acceptance
- Draw a short text label near the hovered tile showing: land height (green), a `+` separator, water depth (blue), an `=` separator, and surface height (white or another contrasting color). Water depth and surface to two decimal places. If water is zero, the water and surface parts can be omitted or shown as zero — your call, but it should be obvious at a glance whether the tile has water.
- **Positioning:** the label is positioned relative to the hovered tile, not the mouse cursor. Default position is above the tile, centered horizontally. The label is clamped to the visible map area — it never extends beyond the map edges. Near an edge, the label stays at the closest allowed position and stops moving even as the hovered tile changes. This means multiple tiles near a corner or edge may share the same clamped label position, and that's fine.
- **Sizing:** the label is a fixed screen size at all zoom levels — it does not scale with zoom and has no minimum zoom threshold. The camera's own zoom limits are sufficient.
- The HUD draws above the water layer and above the grid, but the hover highlight should still be visible. Confirm layer order in your plan.
- Void tiles: show "void" or show nothing — your call.
- New boolean in `ViewSettings`: "Show tile HUD", **default on**, with one checkbox row in the settings panel. Update the help overlay if the setting or any key mapping changes.
- No sim changes, no new key mappings beyond the settings checkbox.
